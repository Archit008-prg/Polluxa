# Part 1 Integration Baseline - Candidate Evidence Pack & Guide

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Candidate Instructions for Part 1 Submission**
> Part 1 assesses your ability to follow technical SOP protocols on the live Polluxa enterprise portal (`sales.polluxa.com`). 
> Below is your step-by-step checklist to complete this section and generate your submission evidence pack.

---

## Step-by-Step Action Checklist & Screenshot Log

### Step 1: Environment Access
- [ ] Open your browser and navigate to `https://sales.polluxa.com`.
- 📸 **Screenshot 1 Requirement**: Capture the landing page / portal login screen displaying the URL `sales.polluxa.com`.

### Step 2: Account Sign-Up & System Navigation
- [ ] Click **Sign in with Google** (fastest route).
- [ ] Complete onboarding by entering placeholder company information (e.g. Company Name: `Assessment Workspace Inc`, Company Size: `10-50`).
- [ ] On the main dashboard, scroll down the left sidebar to **ADD ONS** -> Click **Integration**.
- 📸 **Screenshot 2 Requirement**: Capture the left-hand navigation sidebar with **Integration** highlighted and the main Integrations page loaded.

### Step 3: Initiate Connection Protocol
- [ ] Ensure you are on the default **LinkedIn** tab.
- [ ] Click the primary **+ Connect LinkedIn Account** button.
- 📸 **Screenshot 3 Requirement**: Capture the LinkedIn tab showing the **+ Connect LinkedIn Account** button and the agent list below.

### Step 4: Credential Provisioning
- [ ] In the popup modal, ensure you are on the **Email & Password** tab (or Session Cookie if preferred).
- [ ] Enter your LinkedIn login email and password securely.
- [ ] Click **Connect**.
- 📸 **Screenshot 4 Requirement**: Capture the modal with credentials populated before/during clicking Connect.

### Step 5: Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Approval
- [ ] The portal will display *"Establishing secure connection..."*.
- [ ] Immediately check your mobile device / phone linked to your LinkedIn account.
- [ ] When the LinkedIn security push notification challenge appears on your phone, tap **Yes** to approve the handshake.
- 📸 **Screenshot 5 Requirement**: Capture the *"Establishing secure connection..."* screen along with a photo or screenshot of the MFA approval prompt on your phone.

### Step 6: Agent Risk Configuration (Crucial)
- [ ] Once the handshake succeeds, the *"Set up LinkedIn agent"* window will launch.
- [ ] Select your exact LinkedIn Account Age tier:
  - `< 1 Month` (Very High Risk: 5 invites / 10 msgs / day)
  - `1 Month` (High Risk: 10 invites / 15 msgs / day)
  - `2–6 Months` (Moderate Risk: 15 invites / 25 msgs / day)
  - `6–12 Months` (Low Risk: 25 invites / 40 msgs / day)
  - `1+ Year` (Minimal Risk: 30 invites / 60 msgs / day)
- [ ] Click **Next** to deploy the agent.
- 📸 **Screenshot 6 Requirement**: Capture the tier selection screen with your selected tier highlighted before clicking Next.

### Step 7: Operate the Agent & Capture Live Data
- [ ] Navigate to **LinkedIn** -> **Add Leads** in the left menu.
- [ ] Add a target lead list (e.g., recruiter outreach or sales prospects).
- [ ] Let the agent execute outreach to generate genuine activity data.
- 📸 **Screenshot 7 Requirement**: Capture the active LinkedIn agent running with loaded leads and activity stats.
